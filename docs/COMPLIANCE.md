# Standards, Frameworks and Regulatory Alignment

This project is not a compliance-certification tool. The references below describe principles that informed its design and recommended operational use.

## AWS Well-Architected Framework — Security Pillar

Relevant themes include least privilege, account separation, security governance, threat-informed operation and secure observability.

Official reference:
https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/security-pillar/security.html

## NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

The discovery process primarily supports evidence collection related to Govern, Identify and Detect outcomes, while its outputs may support Respond and Recover planning.

Official reference:
https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

## CIS Amazon Web Services Foundations Benchmark

The project can help identify whether AWS security and logging services are present and where evidence exists, but it does not test every CIS recommendation and must not be represented as a CIS compliance scanner.

Official reference:
https://www.cisecurity.org/benchmark/amazon_web_services

## ISO/IEC 27001:2022

The tool can support risk-based evidence gathering and security monitoring decisions within an ISMS. It does not provide ISO certification or prove conformity.

Official reference:
https://www.iso.org/standard/27001

## ISO/IEC 27002:2022

Relevant control themes include access control, logging, monitoring, information protection and secure operational practices.

Official reference:
https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html

## LGPD — Lei nº 13.709/2018

Generated evidence should be assessed for personal data before sharing. Where personal data is present, operators should apply applicable principles including necessity, security, prevention and accountability.

Official reference:
https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm

## Interpretation rule

References to laws, frameworks or standards mean design alignment or operational relevance only. They must not be interpreted as legal advice, certification, audit opinion or automatic compliance.
